# NEPGEOM-GeoPulse-GLOFRisk
GLOFRisk is an automated climate risk platform tracking glacial lake expansion in the Nepal Himalaya. Using Sentinel-1 SAR radar data and regional anomaly detection, it filters climate noise to flag unstable lakes, maps downstream flood corridors, and models seasonal crop vulnerabilities to protect mountain communities.

The Check-Engine Light for Himalayan Glacial Lakes

The Problem

Climate change is making glaciers in Nepal’s mountains melt really fast. This creates glacial lakes. Some of these lakes are not stable. Can suddenly burst. When they do they cause huge floods that destroy farms, power plants and villages downstream. There are thousands of these lakes and scientists can't keep an eye on all of them.

Our Solution

We built a system that works like a check engine light for the mountains. Of just watching one lake our system looks at how a lake is behaving compared to its neighbors in the same river basin.

How it Works

Step 1: Radar Vision. We use satellite radar to see through clouds and track changes in lake areas every month during monsoons.

Step 2: Peer Review. If most lakes in an area are stable but one is growing fast our system flags it as unusual. This helps us ignore melting and find lakes that are becoming dangerous.

Step 3: Predicting the Path. Once a lake is flagged the system maps where water would flow if it burst. We look at farmland and crop cycles downstream to see what and who is at risk.

Why It Matters

In May 2024 a big flood hit the village of Thame. When we used our system on data, from that flood it showed that the lake was sending warning signals months before.

What We Deliver

Interactive Map. A simple dashboard to see lakes grow or shrink over time.

Priority Watch-List. A list of the 5 most dangerous lakes that need attention.

Risk Score. A number that shows how dangerous a lake is based on its growth and people living below it.
